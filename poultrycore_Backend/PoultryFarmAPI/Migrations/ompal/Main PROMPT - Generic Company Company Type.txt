You are a senior full-stack software engineer working on our existing PoultryMaster / multi-business management platform.

The platform already supports multiple companies under one owner/admin account. We already have specialized company types such as Poultry Company and Water Company. We now want to implement a third company type called:

Generic Company

The Generic Company module should be usable by almost any business type, including but not limited to:

- Restaurant
- Hotel / Guest House
- Retail Shop
- Supermarket
- Provision Store
- Boutique / Clothing Store
- Salon / Barber Shop
- Pharmacy
- Hardware Store

- Service Business
- Small Factory
- General Trading Business
- Any other small or medium-sized business

The purpose of the Generic Company module is to provide universal business operations tracking for companies that do not require a specialized poultry or water-company module.

The Generic Company should help a business owner answer these daily questions:

1. How much did we sell today?
2. How much did we spend today?
3. How much profit did we make today?
4. How much cash do we have?
5. Who owes us money?
6. Who do we owe?
7. What products or services are selling most?
8. Which expenses are increasing?
9. What stock is running low?
10. Which staff member handled sales, cash, or expenses?

12. Are there suspicious cash differences, stock differences, or unapproved changes?

Build this as a production-quality generic business management module.

====================================================

Add or confirm support for the following company types:

- Poultry
- Water
- Generic

When CompanyType = Generic:
- Show the Generic Company sidebar.
- Show Generic Company dashboard.

Daily closing checklist:
1. Confirm total sales
2. Confirm total expenses
3. Confirm customer payments
4. Confirm supplier payments
5. Confirm purchases
6. Count cash at hand
7. Confirm MoMo/bank collections
8. Confirm customer credit sales
9. Confirm inventory adjustments
10. Record manager notes
11. Submit to owner/admin
12. Owner/admin approves

ExpectedCash formula:
ExpectedCash = OpeningCash + TotalCashIn - TotalCashOut

CashDifference formula:
CashDifference = ActualCashCounted - ExpectedCash

Once approved:
- Lock the daily closing.
- Prevent edits without correction reason.
- Create audit logs.

26. KEY CALCULATIONS
====================================================

Sales total:
LineTotal = Quantity * UnitPrice - DiscountAmount
Subtotal = Sum(LineTotal)
TotalAmount = Subtotal - DiscountAmount + TaxAmount
Balance = TotalAmount - AmountPaid

Gross profit by sale:
GrossProfit = SalesRevenue - CostOfGoodsSold

Net profit:
NetProfit = TotalIncome - TotalExpenses

Profit margin:
ProfitMargin = NetProfit / TotalIncome * 100

Customer balance:
CustomerBalance = TotalDebits - TotalCredits

Supplier balance:
SupplierBalance = TotalCredits - TotalDebits

Inventory value:
InventoryValue = CurrentStock * CostPrice

Cash at hand:
CashAtHand = Sum(CurrentBalance of active cash accounts)

Expected cash:
ExpectedCash = OpeningCash + TotalCashIn - TotalCashOut

Cash difference:
CashDifference = ActualCashCounted - ExpectedCash

Payroll net pay:
NetPay = BasicPay + DailyWage + Commission + Bonus - Deductions
