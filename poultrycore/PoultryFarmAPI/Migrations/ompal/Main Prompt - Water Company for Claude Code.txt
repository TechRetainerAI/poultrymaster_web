You are a senior full-stack software engineer working on our existing PoultryMaster / multi-business management platform.

We have already upgraded the platform so that one owner/admin account can create and manage multiple companies. A company can be a Poultry Company, Water Company, or future business type. The selected company type should control which modules and sidebar menus are shown.

We now want to build the FULL VERSION of the Water Company module for Ghanaian sachet-water businesses.

Business Context:
A typical Ghana water company pumps water from a borehole, treats it, uses sachet-water machines to produce sachets, packages 30 sachets into one bag, stores finished bags, loads trucks and motor kings/tricycles for distribution, sells to nearby stores, market women, wholesalers, and walk-in customers, tracks credit sales, collects cash/MoMo/bank payments, pays expenses, and reviews weekly/monthly income vs expenditure to determine profit.

This module must go far beyond simple accounting. It should help the owner answer these daily questions:

1. How many bags did we produce?
2. How many bags did we sell?
3. How many bags are currently in stock?
4. Where is the cash?
5. Who owes us money?
6. Which driver/route/customer is making or losing money?
7. Are there shortages, waste, theft, or unexplained losses?
8. What is our weekly/monthly profit?
9. Which expenses are eating into profit?
10. Which machines, vehicles, or raw materials need attention?

Build this as a production-quality business module.

Important Unit Definitions:
- Main product: Sachet Water Bag
- Default conversion: 1 Bag = 30 Sachets
- Currency: GHC
- Business Type: Water Company / Sachet Water Production
- Vehicles may include trucks, motor kings, tricycles, vans, motorbikes.

High-Level Architecture Requirement:
The app already supports multiple companies. Every Water Company table must be company-scoped using CompanyId. No water-company data should leak across companies. Users should only access water-company records for companies they belong to.

Add CompanyType support if not already cleanly implemented:
- Poultry
- Water
- Other

When the selected company has CompanyType = Water, show the Water Company sidebar/menu and modules. When CompanyType = Poultry, show poultry modules.

22. REPORTING CALCULATIONS
====================================================

Implement these calculations:

Total Sachets:
TotalSachets = Bags * SachetsPerBag

Production Cost Per Bag:
ProductionCostPerBag = TotalProductionCost / BagsProduced

Gross Profit:
GrossProfit = SalesRevenue - ProductionCost

Net Profit:
NetProfit = TotalIncome - TotalExpenses

Profit Margin:
ProfitMargin = NetProfit / TotalIncome * 100

Driver Shortage:
ExpectedCash = BagsSold * SellingPricePerBag
TotalAccountedFor = CashCollected + MoMoCollected + BankCollected + CreditSalesAmount
Shortage = max(ExpectedCash - TotalAccountedFor, 0)

Stock Reconciliation:
ExpectedClosingStock = OpeningStock + Produced - Sold - Damaged - LoadedOut + Returned +/- Adjustments

Route Profit:
RouteProfit = RouteSales - FuelCost - DriverCommission - LoadingCost - DamagesValue - Shortages

Customer Balance:
CustomerBalance = TotalDebits - TotalCredits

Cash At Hand:
CashAtHand = Sum(CurrentBalance of active cash accounts)
