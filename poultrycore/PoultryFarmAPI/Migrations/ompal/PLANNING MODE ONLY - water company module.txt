This water company module can be made very impressive because it is not just “income and expenses.” We can design it around the **real operating life of a Ghana sachet-water business**: borehole water pumping, machine production, packaging into 30-sachet bags, inventory movement, truck/motor king distribution, credit sales, cash collection, expenses, and weekly/monthly profit reporting.

The report you shared already tracks the main financial summary: **income, expenditure, and surplus/deficit**. In one uploaded report for Great Favour Natural Mineral Water, the business recorded **GHC 1,189,708.00 income**, **GHC 788,663.93 expenditure**, and **GHC 401,044.07 surplus** for September 2023 to August 2024, with an average monthly surplus of **GHC 33,420.34**.  The app should go far beyond that by showing exactly **where the money came from, where it went, who sold what, which route performed best, and whether production matched sales**.

# 26. Key Calculations

## Bags to Sachets

```text
Total Sachets = Number of Bags × 30
```

## Production Cost per Bag

```text
Production Cost per Bag =
(Packaging Cost + Labor Cost + Electricity/Fuel + Water Treatment + Machine Cost Allocation) / Bags Produced
```

## Gross Profit per Bag

```text
Gross Profit per Bag = Selling Price per Bag - Production Cost per Bag
```

## Route Profit

```text
Route Profit =
Route Sales - Fuel Cost - Driver Commission - Loading Cost - Damaged/Returned Value
```

## Driver Shortage

```text
Driver Shortage =
Expected Cash - Actual Cash - Approved Credit Sales
```

## Stock Reconciliation

```text
Expected Closing Stock =
Opening Stock + Production - Sales - Damages - Loaded Out + Returns
```

## Profit

```text
Profit = Income - Expenses
```

## Profit Margin

```text
Profit Margin = Profit / Income × 100
```
