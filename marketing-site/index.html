Frequently asked questions

Can I run more than one business?＋

Yes. That's the whole idea. Create a Business Office and add as many companies as you like — water, poultry or any generic business — all under one login.

What is the Organization Code?＋

A short code you choose for your Business Office (e.g. EVANSBIZ). You and your staff use it at sign‑in so everyone always enters the right organization.

Does it work on a phone?＋

Absolutely. VisibilityCore is built mobile‑first, so you and your staff can record sales, stock and deliveries from any phone.

Can my staff have limited access?＋

Yes. Add employees once and assign them to specific companies with the right permissions — they only see what they should.
